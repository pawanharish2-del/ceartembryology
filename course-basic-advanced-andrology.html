Diagnostic & Therapeutic Andrology Overview

This course bridges standard pathological semen analysis with high-level clinical ART laboratory requirements. Trainees progress from initial sample liquefaction and macroscopic screening to advanced computer-assisted sperm analysis (CASA), multi-layer discontinuous gradient separations, and diagnostic sperm DNA fragmentation index (DFI) testing.

Crucially, candidates also gain hands-on training in handling challenging clinical samples—such as cryptozoospermic, severe asthenozoospermic, and testicular biopsy specimens (TESA/micro-TESE) retrieved at Kulki IVF Centre.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Zoology, Life Sciences, or Genetics.
  • ✓ Medical Laboratory Technicians (DMLT / BMLT / MMLT) and Pathology laboratory professionals.
  • ✓ MBBS, BDS, BHMS, BAMS, and clinicians seeking specialized andrology diagnostic training.
  • ✓ Practicing embryology assistants and IVF technicians wishing to upgrade to advanced sperm selection methods.

6-Module Modular Syllabus

Structured under WHO 6th Edition benchmarks to cover diagnostic evaluation, therapeutic sperm wash, and cryobanking.

MODULE 01

Macroscopic & Microscopic Semen Evaluation

Liquefaction kinetics, volume, viscosity, pH testing, round cell discrimination, wet mount screening, and agglutination classification.

MODULE 02

Sperm Concentration & Strict Morphology

Neubauer & Makler chamber calculations, progressive motility grading, and Papanicolaou / Spermac Kruger strict morphology staining.

MODULE 03

Sperm Preparation for IUI, IVF & ICSI

Density gradient centrifugation (DGC 45%/90%), direct swim-up from pellet, wash-and-centrifuge, and viscous sample mucolysis.

MODULE 04

Advanced Functional Diagnostics (DFI/ROS)

Sperm Chromatin Dispersion (SCD) halosperm assay, DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI) scoring, Vitality Eosin-Nigrosin, and HOS testing.

MODULE 05

Severe Infertility & Surgical Retrieval

Centrifugation protocols for cryptozoospermia, TESA/PESA testicular tissue mechanical mincing, erythrocyte lysis, and motility stimulators.

MODULE 06

Semen Cryobanking & QA/QC Protocols

Cryoprotectant dilution, slow-freezing vapor phase protocols, post-thaw recovery yield, donor quarantine guidelines, and WHO QA/QC logs.

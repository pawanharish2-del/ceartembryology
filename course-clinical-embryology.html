Comprehensive Course Overview

The Certificate Course in Clinical Embryology is designed for life scientists and clinicians seeking complete mastery over modern assisted reproductive technology laboratories. The curriculum spans every phase of clinical embryology, starting from cleanroom air quality control to embryo culture, gamete cryopreservation, and witness documentation.

Through structured bench tutorials, simulated OPU scanning, dish preparation under oil, and clinical case shadowing at Kulki IVF Centre, students develop the practical reflexes and theoretical rigor needed to operate independently in accredited ART centres.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ M.Sc. or B.Sc. in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Zoology, Genetics, or Life Sciences.
  • ✓ MBBS, MD/MS (OBGYN), BDS, BAMS, BHMS, or Allied Health Science graduates seeking ART laboratory specialization.
  • ✓ Practicing laboratory technicians and junior embryologists seeking formal clinical embryology certification.
  • ✓ Candidates aiming to meet ART regulatory guidelines for assisted reproductive technology laboratories.

6-Module Modular Syllabus

Structured sequentially to build foundational understanding into independent clinical embryology bench execution.

MODULE 01

Reproductive Physiology & Gametogenesis

Oogenesis, spermatogenesis, follicular dynamics, hormonal regulation of cycle stimulation, and gamete transport biology.

MODULE 02

IVF Cleanroom Design & Environment

ISO Grade-A/B cleanroom air handling, HEPA/VOC filtration, positive pressure maintenance, VOC monitoring, and sterile gowning protocols.

MODULE 03

OPU Processing & Gamete Handling

Follicular fluid scanning under stereomicroscopes, cumulus-oocyte complex (COC) grading, dish washing, and denudation timing.

MODULE 04

Insemination Protocols & Culture

Conventional IVF insemination, micro-drop dish preparation under oil, sequential culture media, and tri-gas benchtop incubator balancing.

MODULE 05

Embryo Morphological Grading

Day 1 fertilization checks (2PN/syngamy), Day 2-3 cleavage dynamics, Day 5 blastocyst expansion, and Gardner grading criteria.

MODULE 06

Embryo Transfer Logistics & QA/QC

Catheter loading mechanics, simulated mock transfer, witness documentation logs, key performance indicators (KPIs), and accreditation norms.

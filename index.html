About CEART

A Premier Institute for Clinical Embryology

CEART (Centre for Embryology and Assisted Reproductive Technology) is dedicated to advancing education, practical learning and professional development in clinical embryology and reproductive science. Our approach combines scientific fundamentals, laboratory-oriented learning and clinical exposure to help students understand the principles and practices of modern assisted reproduction. At CEART Jaipur, our focus is on developing scientific understanding, technical awareness, professional ethics and laboratory quality practices. Through structured training and exposure to an IVF clinical environment, we strive to support the development of competent, confident and responsible future professionals in reproductive healthcare. Our mission is to make specialized embryology education accessible, relevant and aligned with the evolving needs of the ART field.

Practical Learning

Learn Beyond the Classroom

Clinical exposure is an important component of embryology education. Through our in house Kulki IVF Centre, CEART aims to connect scientific learning with the clinical environment of assisted reproduction. Depending on the program, eligibility and permitted activities, learners may gain exposure to IVF laboratory organization, quality-control principles, gamete and embryo biology, laboratory documentation, and the workflow of assisted reproductive procedures. All practical activities are conducted within the applicable clinical, ethical, consent and supervision requirements. Hands-on procedures depend on the training scope and authorization of the clinical facility.

Audio-Visual Classrooms

High-definition live camera feeds from microscope stages to 4K displays for real-time procedural reviews.

Advanced Embryology Labs

Cleanroom Grade 10,000 certified training spaces equipped with tri-gas incubators and anti-vibration tables.

Medical Support Services

Active clinical OPDs, pathology observation suites, and simulated surgical pickup rooms for full-cycle exposure.

State-of-the-Art Technology

Laser biopsy workstations, time-lapse embryo imaging incubators (EmbryoScope), and automated sperm counters.

Curriculum & Prerequisites

What Will You Learn?

A structured pedagogical framework combining foundational reproductive biology with advanced clinical and laboratory practices.

Core Modules Covered in Our Programs:

  • Foundations of Reproductive Biology: Human reproductive physiology, gametogenesis, fertilization and early embryonic development.
  • IVF Laboratory Fundamentals: Laboratory workflow, equipment awareness, aseptic practices and environmental monitoring.
  • ART Techniques and Principles: The scientific principles of IVF, ICSI, embryo culture and cryopreservation.
  • Quality, Ethics and Documentation: Quality assurance, traceability, confidentiality, patient safety and ethical practice.

(Note: The modules covered will depend on the selected course and its approved curriculum.)

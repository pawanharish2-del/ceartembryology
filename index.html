About CEART Jaipur

A Premier Institute for Clinical Embryology

CEART (Centre for Embryology and Assisted Reproductive Technology) is dedicated to advancing education, practical learning and professional development in clinical embryology and reproductive science.

Our approach combines scientific fundamentals, laboratory-oriented learning and clinical exposure to help students understand the principles and practices of modern assisted reproduction.

At CEART Jaipur, our focus is on developing scientific understanding, technical awareness, professional ethics and laboratory quality practices. Through structured training and exposure to an IVF clinical environment, we strive to support the development of competent, confident and responsible future professionals in reproductive healthcare. Our mission is to make specialized embryology education accessible, relevant and aligned with the evolving needs of the ART field.

Learn Beyond the Classroom

Clinical exposure is an important component of embryology education. Through our in-house Kulki IVF Centre, CEART aims to connect scientific learning with the clinical environment of assisted reproduction.

Depending on the program, eligibility and permitted activities, learners may gain exposure to IVF laboratory organization, quality-control principles, gamete and embryo biology, laboratory documentation, and the workflow of assisted reproductive procedures.

IVF Laboratory Organization

Understand cleanroom zoning, laminar airflow protocols, positive air pressure systems, and daily laboratory workflows.

Quality-Control Principles

Practical grounding in VOC filtration, CO2 and O2 incubator gas calibration, temperature mapping, and air quality standards.

Gamete & Embryo Biology

Clinical observation of follicular fluid scanning, gamete handling, fertilization assessments, embryo culture, and vitrification.

Documentation & Workflows

Standardized patient witnessing, chain-of-custody tracking, consent documentation, and ethical compliance under National ART guidelines.

Clinical, Ethical & Supervision Notice: All practical activities are conducted within the applicable clinical, ethical, consent and supervision requirements. Hands-on procedures depend on the training scope and authorization of the clinical facility.

Comprehensive Curriculum

Excellence in Clinical Embryology & ART Education

CEART (Centre for Embryology & Assisted Reproductive Technology) is established as an advanced training hub to bridge the acute deficit of certified embryologists. Clinical embryology requires intense motor coordination, understanding of cellular morphology, and unwavering commitment to sterile laboratory protocol.

Core Modules Covered in Our Programs:

  • Semen Processing & Advanced Andrology: Sperm preparation using density gradient centrifugation, swim-up methods, vitality staining, and hypo-osmotic swelling test (HOS).
  • Oocyte Handling & Denudation: Stripping cumulus cells enzymatically and mechanically with precision micropipettes.
  • Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI): Alignment of holding pipettes, selecting morphologically normal spermatozoa, polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) manipulation, and oolemma rupture technique.
  • Embryo Culture & Blastocyst Grading: Gardner grading systems for day-3 cleavage embryos and day-5/6 blastocysts, continuous monitoring, and medium renewal.
  • Cryopreservation & Vitrification: Rapid freezing of oocytes and blastocysts using high-efficiency open and closed carrier systems.
  • IVF Lab QA, QC & Cleanroom Maintenance: HEPA filtration protocols, volatile organic compound (VOC) monitoring, and alarm validation.

Eligibility Criteria:

Candidates with MBBS, MD/MS (Obstetrics & Gynaecology), BAMS, BHMS, or Postgraduates (M.Sc / M.Tech / Ph.D) in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Zoology, or Life Sciences are eligible to enroll for our fellowship and specialized modular programs.

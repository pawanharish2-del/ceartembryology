IN-HOUSE CLINICAL HUB

Our Core Clinical Hub: Kulki IVF Centre

Where classroom reproductive science directly meets active clinical workflows.

CEART Jaipur operates in seamless affiliation with Kulki IVF Centre, providing trainees with a rare and valuable dual-environment learning experience. Rather than training solely in theoretical or simulated settings, our students observe real clinical cycles, understand live patient sample handling protocols, and witness the daily clinical discipline required in an active reproductive healthcare facility.

Advanced Cleanroom Infrastructure

Grade-A laminar flow workstations, positive air pressure cascades, multi-gas benchtop incubators, and real-time VOC carbon filtration matching stringent national ART regulatory benchmarks.

Individual Workstation Practice

Guaranteed hands-on time with dedicated Narishige and Eppendorf micromanipulators and high-resolution stereozoom microscopes ensuring zero queueing and maximum practical dexterity.

Real-world Clinical Immersion

Direct structured observation of active ovum pick-up (OPU), oocyte denudation, ICSI injection, embryo grading, and liquid nitrogen vitrification under senior consultant embryologists.

LABORATORY HARDWARE

State-of-the-Art Training Benches

Every CEART student works on real clinical hardware, mirroring the precise setups found in top-tier IVF institutions worldwide.

01

ICSI & Micromanipulation Suite

Dedicated Narishige and Eppendorf micromanipulators with anti-vibration air tables, heated glass stages, and micro-injection oil pistons for single-sperm immobilization and micro-injection practice.

Dedicated Workstations
02

Andrology & CASA Diagnostics

Computer-Assisted Semen Analysis (CASA) rigs, phase-contrast optics, Makler counting chambers, and density gradient separation stations for comprehensive semen preparation training.

WHO 6th Edition Protocols
03

Vitrification & Cryo Deck

Liquid Nitrogen (LN2) handling stations, Cryotop / Cryolock loading practice, equilibrium and vitrification media equilibration, and rapid immersion cooling techniques for oocytes and blastocysts.

Cryotop & Cryolock Ready
04

4K Digital Multi-Viewing Arena

Microscope digital optical relays streaming high-definition live feeds to ultra-HD displays, allowing senior faculty to supervise micro-dissection and provide instant procedural feedback.

Live Faculty Relay
CAREER PROGRESSION

From Bench Training to Career Launch

Our structured multi-phase training methodology ensures every student transitions with poise from fundamental biology to active clinical placement.

1

Reproductive Science & QA

In-depth lectures on gametogenesis, culture media osmolality, pH buffering, incubator temperature verification, and cleanroom air quality.

2

Bench Skill Simulation

Extensive hands-on micromanipulation, needle holding, pipette pulling, denudation practice, and mock cryo-loading on research-grade stations.

3

Kulki IVF Clinical Exposure

Observer rotations in active patient cycles, observing real ovum pick-ups, witnessing embryo grading, and mastering clinical documentation.

4

Network Placement Support

Direct connection with our nationwide partner IVF hospitals, resume curation, mock interview rounds, and career counseling.
